About BidNest

BidNest is a front-end student project demonstrating an online auction interface. It includes responsive design, sample product cards, search filtering, countdown timers, and simulated bidding feedback. It does not collect payments or place real bids.

© 2026 BidNest — Student demonstration project. All product names, prices, timers, and bids are samples. No real transactions take place.
